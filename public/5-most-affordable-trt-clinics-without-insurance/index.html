Why Cash-Pay Telehealth is the Best Option

When you use a cash-pay (out-of-pocket) telehealth clinic, you remove the "middlemen" that inflate healthcare costs. You don't have to pay a local clinic's commercial rent. You don't have to pay the salaries of their front desk medical billing staff. You simply pay a highly efficient telehealth platform for the time of their board-certified clinicians, and they use a 503A compounding pharmacy to synthesize and ship the testosterone directly to you at wholesale cost.

Furthermore, because they are not bound by archaic insurance reference ranges, these clinicians can treat your symptoms. If you feel terrible at 350 ng/dL, they will treat you to optimize your levels, rather than denying you care because the insurance company claims 300 ng/dL is "normal."
