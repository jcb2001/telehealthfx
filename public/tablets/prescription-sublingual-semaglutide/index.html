How Sublingual Dissolving Tablets Bypass the Stomach

Traditional oral peptides face a major obstacle: stomach acid and digestive enzymes rapidly break down fragile peptide bonds before they can reach the bloodstream. This is why traditional Rybelsus® pills require extreme fasting protocols (taken upon waking with no more than 4 oz of water and zero food for at least 30 to 60 minutes).

Compounded sublingual tablets solve this problem through vascular mucosal absorption. By allowing the micro-tablet to dissolve under the tongue for 90 to 120 seconds, active GLP-1 molecules diffuse through the highly permeable sublingual mucosa directly into the superior vena cava. This completely bypasses the digestive tract and prevents gastric degradation.
