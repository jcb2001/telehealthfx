2. The Regulatory Framework: Section 503A vs. 503B Compounding

Patient-specific compounding of semaglutide and tirzepatide remains fully authorized under Section 503A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. § 353a) when ordered by a licensed provider for an individual patient. The FDA's proposed restriction on GLP-1s applies exclusively to Section 503B mass-outsourcing facilities, not individualized 503A pharmacy dispensing.

Following the FDA's regulatory proposal on April 30, 2026 regarding the 503B Bulks List, substantial public misinterpretation emerged regarding patient access. Section 503A pharmacies operate under the jurisdiction of State Boards of Pharmacy and are bound by United States Pharmacopeia (USP) General Chapter <797> for sterile injectable preparations.

"The critical regulatory boundary in 2026 is formulation integrity. Under USP <797> and FDA compounding compliance policy, legitimate 503A pharmacies formulate exclusively with pure base semaglutide and tirzepatide active ingredients verified via high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) with 98.0% to 102.0% potency. Patients must avoid unauthorized salt forms such as semaglutide sodium, which fail FDA bioequivalence standards." — Dr. Elizabeth Vance, PharmD, BCPS, Lead Compounding & Clinical Pharmacology Specialist

Analytical Verification Standards for 503A Sourcing:

  • Monograph Purity: Independent Certificate of Analysis (COA) confirming ≥ 98.5% pure base peptide via HPLC testing.
  • Endotoxin Clearance: USP <85> kinetic chromogenic assay verifying endotoxin concentrations under 0.5 EU/mL.
  • Sterility Assurance: USP <71> 14-day membrane filtration incubation confirming absence of microbial growth.
