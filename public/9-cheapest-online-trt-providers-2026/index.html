#2 TRT Nation

$99 / month

TRT Nation operates a highly transparent, no-frills platform. They are a volume-based provider that ships reliably. The $99/mo fee is legitimate and does not require a long-term contract.

Financial Audit Note:

While the pricing is transparent, it is structurally $20 higher per month ($240/year) than Telehealth FX for the exact same pharmaceutical compound.

#3 PeterMD

$99 / month (Conditional)

PeterMD heavily markets a $99/month rate, making them appear identical to TRT Nation and highly competitive with Telehealth FX.

Financial Audit Note:

The $99/month rate is conditional. To secure this price, patients are typically forced to pre-pay for 6 months of treatment upfront ($594). If you desire a standard month-to-month billing cycle, the monthly cost increases.

#4 Matrix Hormones

~$110 / month

Matrix Hormones is well-regarded for individualized care and willing to prescribe complex protocols (including Deca/Nandrolone alongside TRT). However, their pricing is not a simple flat subscription; you pay per vial, meaning your monthly cost fluctuates based on your specific dosage.

#5 PeakPerforMAX

~$120 / month

A reputable clinic with good physician oversight. Their pricing tiers are slightly higher than the budget leaders, sitting around the $120 mark depending on the specific protocol selected.

#6 Viking Alternative Medicine

~$130 / month

Viking is a longstanding player in the online TRT space. While their clinical care is robust, their baseline medication and shipping costs average out to a higher monthly liability than newer, more streamlined telehealth platforms.

#7 Defy Medical

A La Carte (High Variable Cost)

Defy Medical operates on an "a la carte" model. You do not pay a monthly subscription. Instead, you pay for the initial consultation (often $150-$250), the follow-up consultations, the proprietary lab work, and you buy the medication directly from their pharmacy. While the care is "concierge" level, the total annualized cost frequently exceeds $2,000.

#8 Marek Health

A La Carte (Premium)

Marek Health is a high-end "optimization" clinic heavily promoted by fitness influencers. They are notorious for requiring massive, highly comprehensive blood panels before initiating treatment. The lab work alone can cost upwards of $400-$600, making them one of the most expensive entry points in the industry.

#9 Hone Health

$120 / month + Medication

Hone Health represents the most deceptive pricing model audited. They heavily advertise a $120/month fee.

Financial Audit Note:

The $120/month is strictly a "membership fee" for access to their platform and basic lab testing. The actual testosterone medication is billed separately on top of this fee, pushing the true monthly cost closer to $160-$200+.
